# Market Research: Pakistan Sports Facility Booking

**Scope:** Padel, futsal, indoor/box cricket — venue booking + matchmaking + team challenges
**Compiled:** 23 Aug 2026 (sources 2024–2026). Confidence labels noted; single-source figures marked as directional.

---

## 1. The Padel Boom (2023–2026)

### 1.1 From zero to 350+ courts in ~2.5 years

- Padel was "unheard of in Pakistan until a year ago," wrote Dawn in Aug 2024 — Legends Arena (Khadda Market, DHA Karachi) opened Pakistan's first commercial padel courts around mid-2023. ([Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1849829))
- Multiple 2025–26 sources consistently report **350+ padel courts and ~50,000 amateur players** nationally — a court count comparable to the entire USA. ([PadelNation UK](https://padelnation.uk/blog/2025/10/06/pakistan-set-for-first-international-padel-tournament/), [meatechwatch](https://meatechwatch.com/2025/12/23/100000-premier-padel-league-of-pakistan-to-launch-in-january-2026/))
- Per-city split (state news agency APP — single source, treat as directional): **100+ padel clubs in Lahore, 60+ in Karachi, ~60 across Islamabad/Rawalpindi**. A separate aggregator claims Karachi has 150+ active courts. ([APP](https://www.app.com.pk/sports/padel-tennis-a-new-sporting-wave-rising-in-pakistan/), [Padelup](https://padelup.pk/blogs/padel-guide/padel-in-pakistan-the-fastest-growing-sport-how-to-start-playing))
- Hubs: Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad/Rawalpindi, Faisalabad; Multan and Peshawar catching up. ([Padel Cafe PK](https://padelcafe.pk/blogs/padel-courts-in-pakistan))

### 1.2 Demand signals

- Legends Arena Karachi: **~750 visitors on weekdays, 1,000+ on weekends** as early as mid-2024. ([Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1849829))
- Peak-hour scarcity: most bookings fall 6–9 PM; Islamabad clubs (Padel Pit) operate until **3–4 AM**. ([Legends Arena](https://legendsarena.pk/), [APP](https://www.app.com.pk/sports/padel-tennis-a-new-sporting-wave-rising-in-pakistan/))
- **Ramadan is a super-season**: players book after iftar and play until suhoor; The Pad's 2025 Ramadan tournament drew 50+ teams across all-girls, mixed doubles, and advanced categories. "Padel is a social hub… linked more with the lifestyle." ([Arab News PK](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2594211/sport))

### 1.4 Economics & institutional momentum

- Court build cost: Rs 4–6M standard, ~Rs 10M international-standard. At Rs 5,000–8,000/hr a well-located court can gross Rs 1.5–2.5M/month at high occupancy → ~1–2 year payback (estimate; global benchmarks say 2–5 years). ([Dunya Digital](https://dunyadigital.co/sports/courts-of-change-the-rise-of-padel-in-pakistan-s-big-three), [Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1849829), [Padel Structure](https://www.padelstructure.com/are-padel-courts-profitable-4070/))
- Government monetizing the boom: CDA auctioned 5 Islamabad padel sites (Jun 2025) for **Rs 7.6M/month total rent**, each winner obliged to build 2 courts in 45 days; Pakistan Sports Board building courts at Jinnah Sports Complex and National Coaching Centres; LDA building Lahore's first government-backed courts. ([Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1920644), [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2025/10/15/lahore-to-get-its-first-government-backed-padel-courts/))
- Professionalization: FIP-affiliated Pakistan Padel Federation; first international tournament (Karachi, Oct 2025); **Premier Padel League of Pakistan launched Jan 2026 — Rs 30M prize fund**, franchises selling at Rs 2.5M+, streamed by myco; even the PCB installed padel courts at the National Cricket Academy. ([meatechwatch](https://meatechwatch.com/2025/12/23/100000-premier-padel-league-of-pakistan-to-launch-in-january-2026/), [The Nation](https://www.nation.com.pk/23-Dec-2025/premier-padel-league-pakistan-debut-2026-rs30m-prize-pool), [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/sport/cricket/pakistan-cricket-introduces-padel-courts-to-boost-player-fitness-and-performance-1.500608951))

## 2. Futsal Landscape

- Growth driven by small-format spaces making football viable in dense urban areas; National Futsal Cup ran as a 5-city circuit (2023–24); Pakistan made its AFC futsal debut Oct 2025. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Futsal_in_Pakistan))
- Supply is poorly documented — directories undercount because most futsal happens at multi-sport arenas and DHA facilities. **Estimate: 40–80+ bookable commercial futsal/turf venues per major city.** DHA Karachi alone runs 4 futsal grounds plus 2 club courts and an academy. ([DHA Karachi](https://www.dhakarachi.org/clubs/sports-club/facilities/))
- Consolidated operators emerging: **FR Sports (Futsalrange): 7 venues / 26 courts** across Lahore & Sialkot, open 24/7, incl. "Pakistan's tallest indoor courts at 43 ft." ([FR Sports](https://futsalrange.com/))
- **Pricing:** FR courts Rs 1,350–3,850/hr; Karachi turfs (Urban Turf, Bahadurabad) Rs 2,500–3,500/hr with day/night split pricing; KhelPoint listings Rs 1,200–3,500/hr.
- **Peak culture:** venues are 24/7 businesses with floodlit night play; **Ramadan flips demand to midnight–sehri** — the Karachi City FC Ramadan Futsal Championship (Bank Alfalah Legends Arena) is in its third season with 32 teams from Lahore to Gwadar, sponsored by Tapmad, Bank Alfalah, GWM, OPTP. ([Legends Arena](https://legendsarena.pk/blog/karachi-city-fc-ramadan-futsal-championship-returns-for-its-third-season/))

## 3. Indoor / Box Cricket & Nets

- "Indoor cricket has exploded in popularity across Karachi" — the street/tape-ball game formalized into caged, floodlit turf arenas. Karachi's Indoor Cricket League ran Season 1 at Legends Arena (Aug 2025), Season 2 in Ramadan 2026. ([Indoor Crickets](https://indoorcrickets.com/best-indoor-cricket-places-in-karachi/))
- **Tape-ball is the demand engine**: ubiquitous year-round; Ramadan night tournaments "pop up in most neighbourhoods," midnight-to-sehri, 4–6-over formats. Box-cricket arenas are effectively monetized tape-ball — moving games from streets to bookable venues. ([Aaj English/AFP](https://english.aaj.tv/news/30318484/pakistan-street-cricket-comes-to-life-after-dark-during-ramadan), [Arab News PK](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2282941/pakistan))

## 4. Demographics & Digital Readiness

- **Population 256M** (Oct 2025), median age **20.6**, 38.8% urban; **64% under 30**, ~100M youth expected by 2030. ([DataReportal Digital 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-pakistan), [UNDP via Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1405197))
- **Internet users 117M (45.6%)**; 194M mobile connections (79.5% broadband-capable); 161M broadband subscriptions; 95% of the network 4G-enabled. ([DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-pakistan), [PTA](https://www.pta.gov.pk/category/pta-celebrates-landmark-achievement:-pakistan-surpasses-200-million-telecom-subscribers-1561058794-2025-06-20))
- **Smartphones: 71.6% of connections ≈ ~140M devices.** ([Economic Survey 2025-26 via PhoneWorld](https://www.phoneworld.com.pk/pakistan-smartphone-usage-71-percent-economic-survey-2026/))
- **Social media: 79.9M user identities (+25% YoY)** — TikTok 79.9M, YouTube 54.3M, Facebook 52.9M — a strong proxy for app-adoption capacity among urban youth. ([DataReportal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-pakistan))
- **Payment rails ready:** Easypaisa 59M registered / 20M MAU; JazzCash ~20.6M monthly users; Raast connects all banks/wallets free; digital channels are 88% of retail banking transactions by volume. Sports platforms (KhelPoint, Kheylo) already accept these rails.

## 5. Spending Power — Who Actually Pays

- National recreation spend is only ~1.1% of household expenditure (HIES 2024-25) — **the market is the urban affluent-youth niche, not the median household**. Urban average household income nearly doubled in six years to Rs 96,767/month; big-city households spend disproportionately on dining/entertainment. ([Weekly Cutting Edge](https://weeklycuttingedge.com/household-survey-2024-25-an-eye-opener/))
- That niche demonstrably pays: Rs 8,000/hr evening padel split 4 ways = Rs 2,000/person (a casual-dining bill), and venues still see 1,000+ weekend visitors. Premium urban gyms charge Rs 15,000–45,000/month. ([Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1849829), [Express Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2485805/fitness-dreams-cost-an-arm-and-a-leg-in-lahore-1))
- Cultural tailwind: youth substituting screen time and dining-out with pay-to-play sports — "this is the very first time they have introduced something other than dining out." ([APP](https://www.app.com.pk/sports/padel-tennis-a-new-sporting-wave-rising-in-pakistan/), [Arab News PK](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2594211/sport))

## 6. The Wedge: How Booking Happens Today

- Dominant channels are **phone calls, WhatsApp, and Instagram DMs** — even premium padel clubs list "phone call preferred"; indoor cricket guides tell players to "contact venues directly via phone." ([BlogPakistan](https://blogpakistan.pk/padel-courts-islamabad-rawalpindi-best-options-prices-locations/), [Indoor Crickets](https://indoorcrickets.com/best-indoor-cricket-places-in-lahore/))
- Even digitized operators lean on WhatsApp: FR Sports uses WhatsApp for OTP and confirmations, bank-transfer payment, 30-minute slot holds.
- New entrants market explicitly against this friction — Kheylo's App Store pitch: **"No more calls, no more confusion."**
- **Matchmaking demand is documented**: Legends Arena staff manually "helped people pair up for matches" (Dawn) — people are doing our Pillar 2 by hand today.

## 7. Market Size (TAM/SAM/SOM sketch — planning estimates)

- **TAM (national, all three sports):** ~350 padel courts + est. 300–500 futsal/cricket venues (~800–1,500 bookable courts). At blended Rs 3,500/hr and 40–55% utilization on 14 bookable hrs/day → **PKR 15–30B/year (~$55–110M) gross facility spend**, growing with court construction.
- **SAM (3 metro regions, digitally bookable):** roughly 60–70% of supply → **PKR 10–20B/year**.
- **SOM (city 1, 24 months):** 150 venues, 30% of their bookings app-originated → **PKR 400–800M GMV/year** flowing through the platform.
- These are derived estimates from the pricing/supply data above, not published figures — refresh with on-ground counts during venue signup.

## 8. Key Takeaways

1. **Timing is exceptional.** Padel went 0 → 350+ courts in 2.5 years; leagues, federations, government land auctions, and sponsor money (PKR 30M prize pools) arrived in the last 12 months. Booking infrastructure has not kept up.
2. **Pricing bands are well-corroborated** (padel 2,500–8,000; futsal 1,350–4,000; cricket 1,500–4,000 PKR/hr) — a solid basis for take-rate modeling.
3. **The wedge is real:** WhatsApp/phone remains the incumbent even at premium venues; venues run 24/7 with 6 PM–3 AM peaks and a Ramadan midnight–sehri super-season across all three sports.
4. **Digital rails are ready:** ~140M smartphones, 40M+ active wallet users, free Raast payments.
5. **Nobody owns matchmaking + team challenges across the three boom sports** — competitors are young, fragmented, single-city or single-sport (see `02-competitor-analysis.md`).
6. **Caveat:** the buyer is the urban top-quintile youth — a niche, but one that fills courts past midnight at Rs 2,000/person. Design and pricing must target this segment; mass-market affordability comes later via cricket/futsal volume.
