# Competitor Analysis

**Scope:** Local Pakistani platforms, regional analogues (India/Gulf), global comparables.
**Compiled:** 23 Aug 2026. Traction figures are self-reported by companies or from cited third parties unless noted.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

- **Pakistan has no dominant player.** The space is a fragmented cluster of very young (2024–2026) apps and websites — PlayPro, KhelPoint, On Spot, PakPlay, Turfy, Kheylo, Padel Chase, Sportefy, BookMySpot, FindSlotz, Spot Arenas, plus venue-owned software (FR Sports, Legends Arena, The Match Point). None shows meaningful public traction: PlayPro, the most feature-complete, has **9 ratings on the App Store**; On Spot has too few ratings to display a score.
- **Closest local threat: PlayPro** — a "Playtomic clone for Pakistan" (open matches, skill levels, tournaments, leaderboards) but padel/racket-centric and tiny.
- **Playtomic already has a beachhead**: at least two Lahore padel clubs (Padel IN Pakistan, Padel One) take bookings through it. If premium padel clubs standardize on Playtomic, the highest-value demand gets locked up.
- **Team-vs-team challenges are genuine white space** — no local platform has them; regionally only Malaeb (Gulf) implements them. This is our clearest differentiator, and it maps naturally onto futsal and tape-ball cricket culture where the unit of play is a standing team.
- **The status quo is WhatsApp/phone/Instagram** — competitors themselves prove the pain point: Turfy finishes bookings on WhatsApp; PlayPro markets "eliminating the need for WhatsApp coordination"; FR Sports sells venues on escaping "the hassle of answering phone calls."

## 3. Local Competitors — Key Profiles

### PlayPro — closest local competitor ([playpro.pk](https://playpro.pk/))
By Nascent Innovations; padel-first. Book a whole court **or create an open match** others join; skill questionnaire at onboarding; leaderboards, rankings, tournaments (Americano/Mexicano formats); player profiles, follow, reviews, in-app messaging. Payments: cash, PayFast (cards+wallets), bank transfer, full or partial payment. Marketing line: create a match "eliminating the need for WhatsApp coordination."
**Weaknesses:** tiny base (9 App Store ratings), racket-sports DNA, no futsal/cricket team features, no team-vs-team challenges.

### KhelPoint ([khelpoint.com](https://khelpoint.com/))
Lahore live, expanding to Karachi/Islamabad. Best payment localization: JazzCash, Easypaisa, cards + KP Coins loyalty. Verified venues; separate owner portal (business.khelpoint.com). Claims 50+ venues, 10k+ bookings, 1,400+ players. **No matchmaking, no teams, no skill system.**

### PakPlay ([pakplay.co](https://www.pakplay.co/))
Widest claimed coverage: 504+ venues, 16 cities (242 cricket, 128 futsal, 123 padel). Web-only, WhatsApp/email confirmations, no integrated payments, no app found. A listings layer, not a transactional platform — but proof venues will sign up (venue testimonial: "60% booking increases").

### Turfy ([goturfy.com](https://www.goturfy.com/))
Deliberately hybrid: shows live availability, holds a slot, then **hands off to WhatsApp; payment direct to venue** ("Turfy doesn't take payment for court bookings"). Free "Venue OS" (bookings, CRM, staff, expenses, analytics). Positioning line — "so you're not juggling screenshots and group chats to find a court" — is direct evidence of the incumbent workflow.

### FR Sports / Futsalrange ([futsalrange.com](https://futsalrange.com/))
Vertically integrated: 26 courts / 7 venues (Lahore, Sialkot), 24/7 booking, WhatsApp OTP/confirmations, loyalty (10 padel hours = 1 free). **Gives its booking software free to third-party venues.** Signal: venue-side software will be given away by someone — don't plan to charge for basic SaaS.

### Playtomic in Pakistan ([Padel IN](https://playtomic.com/clubs/padel-in-pakistan-lahore), [Padel One](https://playtomic.com/clubs/padelone))
Two DHA Lahore clubs already live, running wallet promos (recharge PKR 52,650 → get 60,750 credit). The global #1 has entered passively — counter-position with local payments (JazzCash/Easypaisa/cash-mode), PKR-native pricing, cricket + futsal coverage, Urdu support, and team features Playtomic won't build. Foreign entrants also face Pakistan's 5% Digital Presence Proceeds Tax.

### Long tail
On Spot (Karachi, 11 sports, dynamic-pricing venue dashboard, launched May 2025), Kheylo ("no more calls, no more confusion", JazzCash), Padel Chase (Islamabad, OTP bookings, 6-hour cancellation rule), BookMySpot (261 Karachi facilities listed, app "coming soon"), BookedHours, FindSlotz, Spot Arenas, Sportefy (SSL misconfigured — immaturity signal), Sporty, SukhChayn (Islamabad public parks). **Single-club apps** — Legends Arena, The Match Point — show flagship venues self-solve if no neutral platform wins; a neutral aggregator must offer them *incremental demand*, not just a calendar.

## 4. What to Copy (regional/global playbooks)

### Playtomic (the padel model)
- Three stacked revenue streams: club SaaS (4 tiers), marketplace commission, premium player subs. €29M revenue on €346M volume (TTM Sep 2025), 6,000+ clubs, €110M+ raised, €393M valuation.
- **The real moat is the level system:** ~1.0–7.0 scale seeded by self-assessment, then Elo-like adjustment after every registered competitive match, with a **reliability %** governing how fast levels move. Open matches come in two flavors — *Competitive* (level-gated, affects rating) and *Casual* (open, no impact). Clubs configure open-match slots from the manager side to fill off-peak hours.

### Playo (the multi-sport matchmaking model)
- Join/host games filtered by sport + skill + distance + time; per-sport rating graph and badges; **Karma points** (earned for playing/hosting/reliability, redeemable against bookings); **in-app split payments**; "GameTime" = platform-hosted guaranteed games that solve cold-start liquidity. 5M+ users on <$3M raised.

### Hudle (the venue-acquisition model)
- **"0% commission"** wedge to venues + free partner software; monetizes player convenience fees, subscriptions, leagues. Metro density first (Delhi-NCR), then 75+ cities. Athlete angels for credibility.

### Malaeb (the team-challenge model)
- Only platform anywhere in our research with **create-team → challenge-team** flows, with player profiles carrying stats and reviews. Also the key monetization warning: **pivoted from commission to flat monthly venue subscription** after owner resistance.

### Watch items
- **District by Zomato** added turf/court booking in 10 Indian cities — big-tech aggregators entering the category regionally.
- **Courtsite (Malaysia)** won via facility-management SaaS + a government partnership digitizing national sports complexes — a template for DHA/CDA/cantonment facilities here.

## 5. Three-Pillar Gap Analysis

| Pillar | Local state of the art | Our bar |
|---|---|---|
| **Booking** | Commodity — everyone has some version; real-time + wallet payments only in KhelPoint/PlayPro/Kheylo; most venues still confirm manually | Genuinely reliable real-time calendar (incl. walk-ins), deposits, cancellation-policy engine, dynamic/off-peak pricing tools |
| **Matchmaking** | Only PlayPro (padel, tiny); On Spot claims unverified | Playtomic-grade: casual vs competitive matches, questionnaire-seeded levels that move with results, reliability %; Playo-grade Karma + guaranteed games for cold start |
| **Team challenges** | **Nobody in Pakistan; only Malaeb globally-adjacent** | Team rosters, open + direct challenges, city leaderboards, result confirmation — mapped to futsal/tape-ball standing-team culture |
| **Split payments** | Nobody (PlayPro has partial-payment only) | Per-player share collection via JazzCash/Easypaisa — collecting Rs 500/head from 10 players is exactly the WhatsApp-era pain |

## 6. Strategic Implications

1. **The window is open but closing** — a dozen apps launched 2024–2026, all sub-scale. First credible multi-city, multi-sport execution with real payments consolidates the category.
2. **Fragmentation is by sport and city** (padel apps; Karachi-only apps; Lahore-only apps). Our padel + futsal + cricket scope across the big three cities is broader than any incumbent actually delivers — but sequence it (one city first).
3. **Venue-side willingness to pay is low** — FR and Turfy give software away; Malaeb pivoted. Subsidize venue SaaS early; monetize bookings and players; Playtomic's stacked model is the mature end-state.
4. **Trust/no-shows are unsolved locally** — no marketplace-level reliability scores, deposits, or enforced cancellation policies exist. Solving this is both a product feature and the venue sales pitch.
5. **Adjacent revenue proven for later:** coaching (Khelomore), tournaments/leagues (PlayPro, OpenSports), corporate events (Playo), streaming (MATCHi/Padelgo).
